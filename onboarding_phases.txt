
Uygulamaya bir onboarding (karşılama ve tanıtım) süreci eklemek istiyorum. Bu onboarding süreci, uygulamanın 
dönüşüm oranı açısından çok kritik olacak. Kullanıcıların hem yöntemin hem de uygulamanın değerini hissetmelerini istiyorum. 
Yaklaşık 17-20 sayfalık bir onboarding süreci olmasını planlıyorum. 
Bu süreçte yöntemi tanıtmak, sorular sormak ve uygulamayı kişiselleştirmek istiyorum.

Onboarding bölümleri şunlar olacak:

Gold List Method App — Detailed Onboarding Flow
1. Welcome Screen

Logo: Placeholder for now
Purpose: Create emotional connection and make the user feel they made the right choice.
Headline: “Welcome to your new way of remembering.”
Subtext: “You’ve just chosen a scientifically proven path to mastering vocabulary without stress.”
CTA: “Continue”
Integration: Simple /app/onboarding/welcome.tsx screen.

2. Sign In / Register

Purpose: Allow new users to sign up or existing users to log in.
Headline: “Sign in or create your account.”
Options: Continue with Apple, Continue with Email.
Text: “Already have an account? Log in” and “Skip for now.”
Integration: /app/(auth)/signin.tsx reused. If the user already completed onboarding, skip to the main app.

3. Learn with Science

Purpose: Present the scientific foundation behind the method.
Headline: “Learn with Science.”
Subtext: “The Gold List Method lets your brain naturally remember words. It focuses on long-term memory.”
CTA: “Show me more.”

4. Structured Progress

Purpose: Explain the main process flow of the method.
Headline: “Structured, but stress-free.”
Subtext: “Add words daily, review them every 14 days, and let your memory do the rest.”
CTA: “Continue.”

5. Track, Reflect, Grow

Purpose: Show the app’s analytical and motivational value.
Headline: “Track. Reflect. Grow.”
Subtext: “Get insights on progress, mastery rate, and long-term retention — all automatically.”
CTA: “Continue.”

6. Support & Rating

Purpose: Build emotional connection and gather early feedback.
Headline: “Your support matters.”
Subtext: “We’re a small team building tools for language learners like you. Would you like to rate us later?”
Buttons: “Remind me later” / “Sure.”

7. Science Behind the Method

Purpose: Build credibility and curiosity.
Headline: “The Science Behind It.”
Subtext: “Spacing, relaxation, and natural recall strengthen memory — that’s how the Gold List Method works.”
CTA: “Tell me how it works.”

8. Step 1 – Add Words

Purpose: Explain the first stage of the Gold List process.
Subtext: “Every day, add 10, 15, 20, or 25 words. Just once — no forced repetition.”

9. Step 2 – Wait 14 Days

Purpose: Show the importance of the incubation period.
Subtext: “Let your brain process what you’ve learned naturally. No reviews until 14 days later.”

10. Step 3 – Review and Filter

Purpose: Explain how forgetting and remembering are part of the process.
Main Text: “Magic Starts.”
Subtext: “After 14 days, review your list. Keep forgotten words, archive remembered ones.”

11. Step 4 – Progress Through Rounds

Purpose: Show the longer-term pattern of learning.
Subtext: “Each forgotten word moves to the next round. After four rounds, most words stay permanently.”
Visual: Display round example cards in 3 rows of 4 cards:

Bronze Level: R1, R2, R3, R4

Silver Level: R1, R2, R3, R4

Gold Level: R1, R2, R3, R4
Each card should have its own specific round color.
CTA: “Continue.”

12. Step 5 – What Gold List Method Focuses On

Purpose: Emphasize the philosophy of the method.
Main Text: “When you remember a word, it’s done — it will never be asked again, because you remembered it after 14 days.”
Subtext: “Each forgotten word moves to the next round. After four rounds, most words stay permanently.”
CTA: “Continue.”

13. Personalization Introduction

Purpose: Transition to the questionnaire.
Headline: “Before we start…”
Subtext: “Let’s personalize your learning journey.”

14. User Survey (Multi-Step Carousel)

Purpose: Collect data for a customized experience.
Questions:

Where did you hear about us? (App Store, Friend, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, Other)

Which language are you learning? (dropdown)

What’s your current level? (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2)

What’s your biggest challenge? (Remembering words, Motivation, Time, Consistency)

How’s your long-term memory? (Strong, Average, Needs improvement)

Have you used the Gold List Method before? (Yes, I’m familiar / I’ve heard of it / No, this is new to me)

How many words do you encounter that you don’t know daily? (slider: 10, 100, 100+)

Where do you find new words? (Books, YouTube, Movies, Social Media, Conversations, Lessons)

Why do you want to learn a new language? (Education, Socializing, Finding Love, Moving to Another Country, etc.)
CTA: “Continue.”

15. Customization Loading Screen

Purpose: Simulate AI-driven personalization.
Headline: “We’re setting up your plan…”
Subtext: “Analyzing your input and optimizing your learning schedule.”
Integration: Use Supabase RPC to calculate insights and display a progress bar.

16. Personalized Insights

Purpose: Show personalized results and statistics.
Headline: “Your Personalized Insights.”
Text Example:

“Add 15 words daily → You’ll learn about 3,600 words per year.”

“Your first review day will be in 14 days.”
CTA: “See My Roadmap.”

17. One-Month Learning Roadmap

Purpose: Visualize how the method works over time.
Headline: “Your 1-Month Journey.”
Subtext: “Here’s your first month’s plan — color-coded by activity.”
Color Legend:

Add words: Yellow

Review: Red

Round 2 reviews: Blue

Rest days: Green
Integration: Use calendar view (react-native-calendars or Skia).
CTA: “Continue.”

18. Save Your Journey (Second Sign-In)

Purpose: Encourage users who skipped sign-in earlier to log in before continuing.
Headline: “Save Your Journey.”
Subtext: “Sign in to sync your progress and keep your personalized plan safe.”
CTA: “Sign In / Continue with Google.”

19. Paywall

Purpose: Present subscription and premium options.
Headline: “Invest in Your Language Journey.”
Subtext: “Unlock full access: reviews, streak tracking, AI sentence generation, and lifetime insights.”
Pricing Options: Monthly / Annual / Lifetime
CTA: “Start Learning Now.”

20. Completion Screen

Purpose: End onboarding with motivation and a sense of accomplishment.
Headline: “You’re All Set!”
Subtext: “Welcome to your personalized Gold List journey. Let’s start your first page.”
CTA: “Go to Dashboard.”
Integration: Marks onboarding as complete in the profiles table (has_completed_onboarding = true).
